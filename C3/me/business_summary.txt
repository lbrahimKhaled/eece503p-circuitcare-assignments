CircuitCare is a fictional laptop and consumer-electronics repair service in Beirut, Lebanon. Its mission is to keep useful devices working longer through transparent diagnostics, careful repair, and practical customer education. All names, prices, policies, and contact details in this project are simulated course data, not claims about a real business.

The service desk is open Monday to Friday, 09:00-18:00, and Saturday, 10:00-14:00 (Asia/Beirut). Sunday is closed. Walk-ins are accepted for intake; availability and appointments must be confirmed by a human. The fictional contact address is help@circuitcare.example. No real street address or telephone number has been assigned.

Services and illustrative USD prices: diagnostic assessment $15; internal cleaning $25; operating-system setup $30 (customer must provide a valid licence); SSD installation labour $20; battery replacement labour $20. Parts, licences, and data recovery are excluded. Diagnosis can identify a repair estimate; customers must approve it before work begins. The diagnostic fee is credited against approved repair labour. Standard workshop assessment takes 1-2 business days; neither completion dates nor part availability are guaranteed. Data recovery, soldering, liquid-damage repair prices, brand-authorised status, and specific replacement-part prices are not published: refer these questions to the team.

A 30-day workmanship warranty covers the completed repair labour, excluding accidental damage, new faults, liquid damage, and customer-supplied parts. The service does not guarantee data preservation: customers should back up data before service. Staff never ask customers to send passwords through chat.

The fictional team consists of Rami Haddad, founder and repair technician responsible for diagnostics; Maya Nassar, customer service coordinator responsible for intake and follow-up; and Lina Mansour, quality coordinator responsible for repair checks and repair-before-replace guidance. The unique value proposition is an understandable estimate before repair, a diagnostic-fee credit, and practical advice aimed at extending device life rather than unnecessary replacement.

The chatbot can explain published services and policies, collect a consenting customer's name, email, and issue for follow-up, and record feedback or unanswered questions. It cannot make a final diagnosis, complete a booking, charge a customer, order parts, or promise an exact quotation. For a swollen battery, smoke, sparks, or liquid exposure, stop using and charging the device if safe and seek professional help. Do not open, puncture, heat, or handle a damaged battery.
